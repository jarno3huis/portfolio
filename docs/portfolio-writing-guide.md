# Portfolio Documentation Guide

For every completed project, document the work as if another IT professional needs to understand and reproduce it.

## 1. Scenario

What problem are you solving?

## 2. Requirements

What does the environment need to do?

## 3. Design

Explain your architecture and important decisions.

## 4. Implementation

Explain what you actually configured or built.

## 5. Security

Explain risks, controls and least-privilege decisions.

## 6. Testing

Show how you verified that the solution works.

## 7. Problems

Document errors or unexpected behavior you encountered.

## 8. Resolution

Explain how you investigated and fixed the issue.

## 9. Lessons learned

What did you learn?

## 10. Improvements

What would you change in a production environment?

## Important

Do not claim experience you have not actually gained. Mark unfinished sections as planned or in progress.
